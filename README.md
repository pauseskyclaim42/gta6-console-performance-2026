## 🔥 Why This Exists

GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026. Digital Foundry tested it: base PS5 and Series X hold **stable 30 FPS** in normal gameplay. But in action scenes — shootouts, explosions — frame rate drops to **20–25 FPS**. Series S runs at 1080p/30 with similar dips.

Players are frustrated. One Reddit user wrote: "I'm literally getting nauseous from 30 FPS." Another started avoiding firefights because every gunfight drops to 22 frames.

This companion doesn't fix the game. It helps you adapt.

## 🧩 What It Does

### Scene-Aware Preset Switcher
- Select scenario: Free Roam, Mission with Combat, Chase, Cutscene
- Recommended settings per scene:
  - Combat: HDR off, reduced crowd density, performance mode (if available)
  - Free Roam: HDR on, full density
- Notes from Digital Foundry testing

### Stutter Log
- Manually log drops: time, scene, duration
- Companion shows patterns: worst scenes, average drop length, clustering
- Export to CSV for sharing with Rockstar support

### Platform Comparison Notes
- Base PS5 vs PS5 Pro vs Series X vs Series S
- Digital Foundry data: resolution, frame rate, dip severity
- Notes on what each platform does well

## ❓ FAQ

**Does this improve FPS?**
No — it's a companion, not a performance mod. Rockstar controls optimization. This tool helps you adapt settings and track patterns.

**Does it work on PC?**
GTA 6 is not available on PC. This companion is for console players.

**Will I get banned?**
No — this is a standalone desktop app. It doesn't interact with your console or the game.

**How accurate are the platform notes?**
Based on Digital Foundry's testing at launch. Performance may change with patches.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes — all features are local. No internet required.

**How do I uninstall?**
Delete the folder. No registry entries.
